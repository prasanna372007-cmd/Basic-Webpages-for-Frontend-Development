Nature

About

Nature is the most beautiful and precious gift on Earth. It includes mountains, rivers, forests, animals, plants,and the fresh air around us. Nature provides everything needed for living beings to survive. Nature also plays an important role in maintaining the balance of life on Earth. Trees absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen, while rivers and oceans support countless living organisms. Every part of nature is connected, and even small changes can affect the environment. Because of pollution, deforestation, and climate change, nature is facing many dangers today. It is our responsibility to protect and preserve it by planting trees, reducing waste, and using natural resources wisely.
